# Standards and applicability

Publisher catalog scopes checked 2026-09-26. This is a selection guide, not the normative text or a compliance checklist. Recheck the required edition, amendments and national adoption for the actual project, and use the applicable standard text for implementation and conformance review.

| Reference | Relevance |
| --- | --- |
| [ISO 6412-1:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/73352.html) | General conventions for simplified pipeline drawings, including orthogonal representation. |
| [ISO 6412-2:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/73353.html) | Additional conventions for isometric pipeline representation; used with Part 1. |
| [DIN EN ISO 6412-2:2018-06](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-iso-6412-2/286511626) | German adoption of ISO 6412-2:2017 through EN ISO 6412-2:2018. Adoption dates can differ from the ISO edition. |
| [ISO 129-1:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/64007.html) | Presentation of dimensions and associated tolerances. Check applicable amendments; presentation rules do not supply engineering tolerance values. |
| [ISO 7200:2004](https://www.iso.org/standard/35446.html) | Data fields in technical-document title blocks and headers. |
| [ASME Y14 standards](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/y14-standards) | Drawing/documentation standards to consider when the project specifies an ASME drafting framework; select relevant parts and editions. |

ISO describes its standards as [voluntary](https://www.iso.org/foreword-supplementary-information.html). Determine what the project contract, client specification and applicable requirements actually call for; do not assert a universal legal obligation or that a drafting standard overrides local requirements.

Drawing conventions and piping design rules have separate roles. A drawing's appearance cannot establish design compliance, and a solved model cannot establish drawing completeness. Project specifications may also define line numbering, symbols, materials lists, weld identification, sheet splitting, issue status and approval workflow. Record those choices rather than treating one client's layout as universal.

For a European-oriented Tuba review exporter, ISO 6412 Parts 1 and 2 are a reasonable proposed starting point, subject to the project's chosen conventions. This is a recommendation, not a claim that Tuba already implements those standards. Obtain the full applicable rules before adding a standards-compliant or fabrication-release label.
